Where you are

One hostile starts the combat score; three or more push it to the top tier. Set hostiles back to 0 to hear the fight resolve into the local theme.

Score accents

Ship

Drag shields to zero and back to hear them restore. Hull damage above 70 % raises the alarm, sputters the drive and makes the frame creak and arc.

Events

Distance 0 plays an event as your own ship (full layers, cockpit room). Any distance plays it out in the world: quieter, darker, delayed by range, with more tail. Press a button repeatedly — no two plays are identical.
