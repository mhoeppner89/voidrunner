VOIDRUNNER · AUDIO REVIEW

Music

Every piece of the adaptive score, played live by the game’s own music director. No two plays are identical, because each piece writes its bars as it goes. New pieces reach the game only once they are approved here. Verdicts and notes stay in this browser until you export them.

Hear the game’s choices

This runs the real selection without picking a piece yourself: open space, planets, the belts and the graveyard rotate pieces with silence between them, weighted by star system and nearby region. Docked themes follow the station.

Combat pieces open with one hostile (tier 1). “Three hostiles” escalates to tier 2, and “End the fight” lets the danger fade through tier 0 into the resolve chord. “Hear the ending” shortens a rotating piece to eight bars so you can hear its closing cadence and the rest that follows.
